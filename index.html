Inside Polus

More ways to play.

Designed for custom games and community experimentation.

✦

Custom roles

Original role concepts, individual abilities, and role-specific progress systems for compatible modded clients.

◎

Host tools

Host-authoritative controls intended for private lobbies. Vanilla players do not receive custom role UI or effects.

⌘

Android project

An Android loader and patcher experiment with a separate in-game mode selector and compatibility work in progress.
